# Data Analytics & Machine Learning Projects

A collection of academic projects showcasing data analytics, data visualization, data preprocessing, customer behavior analysis, classification, clustering, and predictive modeling using Python, SQL, and Jupyter Notebook by Amanda Ang. These projects apply analytical and computational techniques to real-world problems across hospitality, retail, product classification, and marine safety, alongside quantitative research into deepfake awareness and reporting behavior.

## Projects

### 1. Hotel Booking Cancellation Prediction

Investigates factors associated with hotel booking cancellations and explores predictive modeling to help INN Hotels Group better understand cancellation patterns and inform cancellation and refund policies.


### 2. Online Retail Data Analysis

Explores transactional data from a UK-based online retailer covering December 2010 to December 2011. The project uses data manipulation and visualization to investigate retail transactions and purchasing activity.


### 3. Product Classification and Clustering

Explores product data from multiple merchants, investigating classification and clustering in the context of product categorization, grouping, and matching.


### 4. Global Shark Attack Analysis

Explores worldwide shark-related incidents using the Global Shark Attack File. The project examines incident information, including locations, human activities, species, and injury outcomes, to investigate patterns relevant to public safety and marine conservation.


### 5. Nata Supermarket Customer Analytics

Analyzes customer demographics, income, family characteristics, product-category spending, promotional participation, and purchasing frequency across online and physical stores to investigate customer behavior and support business decision-making.


## Research

### From Awareness to Action: The Role of Ethical Position and Detection Ability in Deepfake Awareness and Reporting Behavior among Students in Metro Manila

A quantitative study examining the relationships between deepfake awareness, ethical position, detection ability, and reporting behavior among students in Metro Manila. The study uses survey data collected from 60 students and explores how awareness, detection ability, and ethical considerations relate to the willingness to report deepfake content.
